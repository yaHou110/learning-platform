## Summary

Third SPRINT-002 feature slice: security/network hardening follow-ups — real RFC 9116 `security.txt` contact, per-request CSP nonces (no more blanket `unsafe-inline` as the real policy), and a Dependabot config to replace the retired `pnpm audit`.
